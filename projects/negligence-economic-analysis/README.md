# Economic Analysis of Negligence and Liability

This academic paper analyzes how standards of care and liability rules can create incentives for efficient precaution. It compares the Learned Hand framework with marginal analysis and applies economic reasoning to legal case studies.

## Deliverable

[analysis.docx](analysis.docx) is the original paper credited to Patrick Hopes and dated April 27, 2025.

## Analytical focus

The paper defines efficiency, externalities, standard of care, cause, and negligence. It examines precaution cost, accident probability, and expected harm, then discusses United States v. Carroll Towing Co. and Spur Industries, Inc. v. Del E. Webb Development Co.

This project demonstrates structured academic argument, marginal analysis, risk reasoning, and analytical writing. The portfolio describes the paper's scope without treating its interpretations as verified legal conclusions or professional legal advice.
